Overview

A road-and-fly trip through interior and Arctic Alaska — Denali, Gates of the Arctic, Wrangell–St. Elias, and Kobuk Valley — followed by a week cruising Hubbard Glacier, Glacier Bay, and the Inside Passage. Five days in the middle are still open for Lake Clark and Katmai.

Footnotes

  1. Flights to Anchorage from Florida: MIA · FLL · TPA · MCO · RSW
  2. No dedicated weather buffer day for this flight — per the note that missing one flightseeing tour among many isn't a big loss.
  3. Jul 1 is a dual-purpose day: if Jun 30's flight was weathered out, this is the backup attempt (still flying during the day and driving back to Fairbanks that same night); otherwise it's just the drive-back day.
  4. Copper Valley Air Service (Gulkana Airport, Glennallen, ~15 min from Copper Center): "Three Peak Tour" (~60 min, ~$400/person, fly-over of Mt. Sanford, Mt. Drum, Mt. Wrangell, no landing) or "Flight of Majesty" (~120 min, adds mud volcanoes and wildlife spotting). Weather-dependent, but optional and slotted into a non-dedicated day, so no schedule impact if skipped.
  5. Kennicott Glacier Lodge has a 2-night minimum and very limited dining alternatives on-site (their dining room, the Meatza Wagon food truck, or a trip into McCarthy) — a meal package is worth booking.
  6. Kotzebue has no direct flights from Fairbanks — all routes connect through Anchorage. The Kennecott → Anchorage drive happens July 5 (with the Matanuska Glacier stop), and the Jul 6 outbound flight is the early one (~8:40–9am, landing ~10:30am) rather than an afternoon flight — this opens a same-day bonus attempt at Kobuk Valley on top of Jul 7 and Jul 8, giving three possible flying days instead of two. Jul 9 remains a flex morning + late flight back. Flight links: ANC→OTZ · OTZ→ANC
  7. Lake Clark/Katmai structure (Port Alsworth stay vs. Anchorage day trips) is still open — once decided, activities/hotel/restaurant will be filled in the same way as the rest of the trip.
  8. The Whittier tunnel (Anton Anderson Memorial Tunnel) runs one-lane alternating traffic on a schedule, not free-flow — worth checking timing before the drive down so you're not stuck waiting right before boarding.
  9. Cruise Itinerary — port order, arrival/departure times may vary by sailing date; confirm final schedule closer to departure. The two dedicated glacier-viewing days (Hubbard + Glacier Bay) give natural redundancy if weather affects one of them.
  10. Kennecott was compressed from 3 nights to 2 (Root Glacier hike + Mill Tour fit into one full day), which originally freed up July 5 — now used for the Matanuska Glacier helicopter tour en route to Anchorage. The dedicated Wrangell–St. Elias flightseeing day is still the one dropped in this compression; still worth reconsidering during the buffer re-evaluation pass if you'd rather not have skipped it.
